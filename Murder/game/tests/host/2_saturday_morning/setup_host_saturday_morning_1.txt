Chapter: A Shocking Morning
Character: Lady Claythorn
I wake and start getting ready for breakfast.
I realise I have the easy role in all of this.
The rest of the staff must have been at work a big part of the night.
Then awake sooner than I do.
I hope they will still be all right today.
I reach the dining room hoping to be the first one there.
To my surprise, Captain Sinha is already seated, enjoy a light breakfast.
Sushil Sinha: Good morning, I am sorry, I am already.
Sushil Sinha: It is a soldier's habit, I am afraid.
Lady Claythorn: Do not worry Captain.
Lady Claythorn: I will fix myself a plate and join you soon.
I take my place at the table and the guests arrive one by one.
The last one to arrive is Samuel Manning.
That is not a surprise, he looks like he drank himself to sleep last night.
But I do not see Thomas Moody here.
That I did not expect.
The butler enters the room and comes directly towards me.
I did not even noticed he was gone.
He whispers in my ear.
Butler: I think there is something wrong with Thomas Moody.
Lady Claythorn: What do you mean?
Butler: He is still in his bed at this hour.
Butler: That cannot be normal.
Lady Claythorn: Really?
Lady Claythorn: What is wrong with him?
Butler: I am not sure.
Butler: We should ask doctor Baldwin to come and inspect him.
Lady Claythorn: All right.
Lady Claythorn: Doctor Baldwin, I'm sorry to interrupt your breakfast, but would you mind coming with me?
Lady Claythorn: We need your assistance.
Without hesitation, Daniel Baldwin stands up.
Daniel Baldwin: Of course, I'll follow you.
We climb the stairs quickly, the butler a step ahead of me.
Ted Harring follows without being asked, and nobody thinks to send him back.
Butler: Here we are. Thomas Moody's room.
We all step into the room.
Butler: He's here in his bed. He didn't respond when I tried to wake him up.
He lies on his back with the mask still on, and the bedclothes hardly disturbed.
I have played a dozen deathbeds, and not one of them ever looked like this.
The doctor takes him by the wrist.
Daniel Baldwin: No pulse.
Daniel Baldwin: He's dead.
Lady Claythorn: Dead?!!!
Lady Claythorn: How is that possible?
Daniel Baldwin: I'm not sure yet. I'll need to examine him further.
Daniel Baldwin: Best if you stepped out for this.
Daniel Baldwin: I'll return downstairs once I know more.
Butler: Please, My Lady—this way.
Lady Claythorn: Oh... Yes, of course...
The butler closes the door behind us and folds his hands, as calm as if he had just carried out a tray.
A man is dead in that bed, and he is already thinking of the next thing to be done.
That, and not the body, is what frightens me.
Sushil Sinha: Lady Claythorn, what is happening?
Lady Claythorn: I'm sorry to announce such horrible news, everyone.
Lady Claythorn: It appears that Mr Moody passed away in his sleep last night.
Sushil Sinha: Do we know what happened?
Lady Claythorn: Doctor Baldwin is examining him right now.
Lady Claythorn: He will likely tell us more later.
Sushil Sinha: How terrible.
Amelia Baxter: Yes, such sad news.
Lady Claythorn: Doctor Baldwin, can you tell us more about what happened?
Daniel Baldwin: I can't say anything definitive for now.
Daniel Baldwin: But it's very likely a natural death, possibly triggered by an old wound from the war.
Daniel Baldwin: In any case, we need to call the authorities.
Lady Claythorn: Yes, of course.
Lady Claythorn: My butler will take care of it.
Daniel Baldwin: Very well.
We continue eating in a heavy silence.
Nobody speaks much.
What now?
Lady Claythorn: Well, as I mentioned earlier, activities were planned for today.
Lady Claythorn: This morning, those who wished to were supposed to go on a hunt.
Lady Claythorn: A sad event has happened, but it's not a reason to remain idle.
Lady Claythorn: So, if no one objects, I propose that we continue according to what was planned.
A faint murmur of assent passes through the room.
Lady Claythorn: Excellent.
Lady Claythorn: Everything is ready for those who wish to join the hunt.
Lady Claythorn: I know most of you are probably not accustomed to this type of event.
Lady Claythorn: That's why our staff can lend you everything you'll need—clothes, guns—and they will also assist you throughout the event.
Lady Claythorn: Of course, you can choose to simply stay here.
Lady Claythorn: You can relax and enjoy the warmth of the house until the others return.
