# 🥃 Drunk's Story Outline: Samuel Manning

## Perspective & Tone
Samuel Manning is a fifty-five-year-old defence barrister, ruined by drink since his wife Eleanor died under an opioid-addled surgeon. He is the one guest who is not what he plays: behind the incoherence is a sharp legal mind that can sober up when it must, and behind the drink is a palate that can read a kitchen from a single mouthful. His voice on the page is dry, self-lacerating and clear-sighted; the drink slurs the narration (`drunk_mode`), sobriety sharpens it.

- **Primary goal**: survive a weekend engineered to make him kill and then be killed.
- **Key trait**: he watches. His tragedy is that he has spent a lifetime watching things happen and doing nothing. Every ending turns on whether, this time, he acts.

---

## Narrative Arc

### 🚂 Friday Afternoon — `drunk_introduction`
Second class, drunk from the first line. The chapter is a chain of places with black-outs between them: the train, the platform, the car, the hall, the tea room, the sofa. He reads the invitation (defender of the poor, a thousand pounds), and passes out on the tea-room couch, which is where the other guests first see him. Unlocks his own `age`, `status`, `addict`, `job`, `heroic_act`.

### 🍽️ Friday Evening — `drunk_day1_evening`
Seated on Lady Claythorn's left. Dinner is half-seen through the drink, except the **sole**, which he reads whole and sober for one paragraph (`food`) — and which tells the hostess he is not the man he plays. He half-registers the things that come back to haunt him on Saturday night: the butler's fighter's hands, the psychic counting the boy's fingers, the doctor's addict sweat, Moody drinking only from his own flask.

- **Billiard room** (before the others): he carries off every bottle worth the name (`raided_bar`, and the `port` he will need on Sunday).
- **The letter**: on his desk, in a woman's hand, naming the doctor as Eleanor's killer (`wife`, and doctor `fraud`). He writes the burned second half. Then **DRINK or not**:
  - **Drink** (`day1_drink`) — the only option on a first run.
  - **Put the bottle down** — an intuition option, unlocked only after dying `despair`.

### ☀️ Saturday Morning — `drunk_day2_morning`
Two versions. Drank: he wakes remembering nothing, reads the letter as new, finds his own handwriting on it, burns it, and fills the flask because his hands demand it. Sober: first clear morning in years, he burns the letter and chooses the flask — **water with a finger of whisky for the smell** (`watered_flask`, unlocks `lie`) or whisky. Then the shared breakfast: Moody found dead, the hunt announced, the men take rifles.

### 🌲 Saturday Afternoon — The Hunt — `drunk_day2_hunt`
Western grove, with the doctor, the boy and the footman.
- **Whisky flask**: the woods are a blur, the rabbit is real, and he shoots Baldwin without knowing whether he meant to. → `despair` (intuition ending, "kill yourself of sadness").
- **Watered flask**: he sees the doctor's withdrawal sweat (doctor `addict`), the footman-and-doctor tenderness, and at the halt he gets the doctor's back at twenty paces and a choice:
  - **Fire** (`shot_doctor`) — deliberate, called a rabbit. → Saturday evening.
  - **Lower the rifle** (`spared`) — the decent act; someone with a key finishes the night for him.

### 🔒 Saturday Evening — `drunk_day2_evening`
Carried home a killer, accused, locked in the George IV room. The chapter is one long think (`drunk_day2_evening_menu_think`). Three topics make the picture whole — the letter, the dead telephone call (`phone_call`), the butler's face from a dock (`butler_face`) — and unlock `understood`. Then:
- **Play dead** with port for blood and his own razor dropped beneath his hand, so the house reads it as suicide (`played_dead`; needs `understood` + `port`). → Sunday.
- **Confront the house** — nobody believes the drunk who shot the doctor, and the one who does has the key (`silenced`).
- **Accept it and drink** — he cuts his own throat for real; in the morning the house finds exactly what it would have found had he played dead (`throat_cut`).

Whether real or staged, every other route sees the same body: throat cut, razor on the floor beneath his hand, door still locked as the Captain left it. Nobody reads it as murder: it passes for a drunk who could not live with having shot Doctor Baldwin.

### 🌫️ Sunday Morning — `drunk_day3_morning`
Playing dead. The door opens, the Captain takes two steps, pronounces him dead and does not touch him (`faked_death`). He waits out the morning, then slips down the drainpipe once the living have thinned.

### 🎭 Sunday Afternoon — `drunk_day3_afternoon`
Hidden at the serving hatch, he watches the last three (lad, psychic, nurse) lay lunch. The nurse swaps her poisoned plate onto the boy. His one chance to act:
- **Swap the plates back** (`swapped_back`): the poison returns to the nurse, the boy only sleeps. He recognises Amelia Baxter weeping over her son as the client he failed years ago (`lost_case`), watches the butler return for the silver and greet the waking boy, and is gone before the ending finds him (`survived`; unlocks the **lad** and most of the **butler**).
- **Do nothing**: the boy eats the poison and dies, and the returning butler checks the bed the Captain did not (`found_out`).
